# Layer 1 validation, 2026-08-17

**Status:** first execution of `validation/protocol.md`. Layer 1 only.
**Analyzer commit:** `57f6132`
**Controls:** `idd` at `honest-conversion` (positive) and `develop` (negative), cloned rather than checked out, so the source working tree was untouched.

## Bottom line

The positive control passes. The negative control does not, and the reason turns out to be the control rather than the instrument.

| control | Slop signals | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| honest-conversion | 2 of 17 | **passes**, comfortably structured |
| develop | 5 of 17 | **does not score as unstructured** |

The instrument separates the two controls and every discriminating indicator points the right way. It separates them by less than the protocol predicted in April, and the next section is why.

**The investigation the protocol demands has been done, and the second candidate is the answer.** The protocol was written against an eleven-indicator panel and states both expected outcomes "of 11". `spec/03-layer1-indicators.md` now defines twenty indicators and sets the high-confidence threshold itself: *"A codebase scoring in the slop column on eleven or more of the twenty indicators is considered to exhibit the unstructured-condition pattern with high confidence."* The same paragraph excludes n/a indicators from both numerator and denominator.

So "≥ 7 of 11" was never the live threshold for this run; it was a superseded one. The protocol has been repointed at the spec, which is a correction to a document that lagged the methodology rather than a change to the methodology.

**Against the live threshold the picture is unchanged in direction and worse in degree.** Three indicators returned n/a, so the denominator is 17.

| control | slop signals | live threshold | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| honest-conversion | 2 of 17 | 11 of 20 for high-confidence unstructured | comfortably structured |
| develop | 5 of 17 | 11 of 20 for high-confidence unstructured | **does not reach it either** |

The negative control does not exhibit the unstructured-condition pattern by the spec's own current standard. That is the finding, and it is about the control rather than the instrument: `develop` is a better codebase than the protocol assumed in April. A negative control that does not score as unstructured cannot demonstrate that the instrument detects unstructured code, so **this pair does not yet validate the negative direction.** The protocol's own selection criteria list a synthetic repository and a hotspot-selected open-source repository as alternatives, and one of those is now needed.

## What separates, and by how much

Four indicators carry the separation. L1.5 reads 74.7 Healthy against 23.9 Slop. L1.12, unreachable code, reads 0.84 Healthy against 5.87 Slop. L1.17, god-file concentration, reads 0.85 Not Healthy against 3.27 Slop. L1.18, unbounded mutable state, reads 15.1 against 35.6, both Not Healthy but a clear direction.

## What does not separate, and one of them matters

L1.14 reads exactly 6 on both controls. All twelve findings are `generic-credential`, and every one sits in a test file, a documentation page, a CI workflow or a `.env` template. That is by design rather than a defect: the scanner matches `gitleaks detect --no-git` over git-tracked files, and a credential committed to a test file is committed. The consequence for validation is real all the same. L1.14 contributes nothing to separating these two controls, and the slop signal count treats it as one of the twenty.

L1.15 is Slop on both, 17.64 against 18.8, and separates nothing either.

## What was not measured

L1.13 returned n/a because jscpd is not installed on the measuring machine. L1.19 and L1.20 were not run: the audit used `--no-exec`, so neither control's test suite was executed. Layer 2 and Layer 3 are human audits and have not been performed, so this is one layer of three.

## Method

Both controls were cloned with `git clone --single-branch` from the local repository into a temporary directory. The source repository was verified before and after to be on its own unrelated branch with a clean working tree. The analyzer ran at `57f6132` with `--no-exec --format json`.

## Owed

A negative control that scores as unstructured, without which the negative direction is untested. Layers 2 and 3. A re-run with a test-execution trace so L1.19 and L1.20 carry values. And the protocol's own results path, `methodology/validation/results/`, is stale: the tree has been `validation/` at the repository root since the restructure, which is where this sits.
